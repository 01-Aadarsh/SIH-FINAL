**Q: What does Section 3(p) of the Patents Act say about traditional
knowledge?**
*(Jurisdiction: India)*
A: An invention that is, in effect, traditional knowledge — or that just
combines the known properties of traditionally known ingredients — is
not patentable under Section 3(p). Cites Patents Act 1970, Patent Office
Manual. Confidence ~99.7%.

---

**Q: Is a topical paste made of pure turmeric and neem powder patentable
for wound healing?**
*(Jurisdiction: India)*
A: No — the wound-healing/pesticidal properties of turmeric and neem are
documented traditional knowledge, so this counts as an aggregation of
known properties, barred under Section 3(p)/3(e). Confidence ~89%.

---

**Q: Do I need National Biodiversity Authority approval before filing a
patent for an invention derived from Indian sandalwood?**
*(Jurisdiction: India)*
A: You can file the patent before NBA approval, but must obtain NBA's
prior approval before the patent is actually granted/sealed. Cites the
Biological Diversity Act. Confidence ~99.7%.

**Q: What are the grounds on which a Geographical Indication registration
application can be refused in India?**
*(Jurisdiction: India)*
A: Refused if accepted in error, or if it doesn't actually qualify as a
GI under the facts of the case, per the GI Act/Rules. Confidence ~99.8%.

**Q: What are the mandatory disclosure of origin obligations for patent
applicants under the WIPO GRATK Treaty 2024?**
*(Jurisdiction: International — switch it first)*
A: Article 3 requires disclosing the country of origin (or source) of the
genetic resources / traditional knowledge the invention is based on.
Confidence ~95%.

**Q: Can I patent an extract of Ashwagandha, and do I need any
biodiversity clearance first?**
*(Jurisdiction: India)*
A: Yes, clearance is needed — apply to the NBA under Section 19, get
approval (up to 90 days), declare it on Patent Form-1, and be aware TKDL
evidence can be used to oppose a non-compliant application. Confidence
~80%, cites Biological Diversity Act + IPIndia guidelines. Surfaces NBA
Form 7 and Form 8 as actionable forms, and compliance flags for
Section 6, Section 7, and TKDL.
